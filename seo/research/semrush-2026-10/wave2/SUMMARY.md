## Feature long tail

- **The exact feature phrasings have no measurable volume:** forward email, cancellation deadline reminder, scan boarding pass, check-in reminder, multi-currency, export to PDF. Keep them as conversion copy.
- **The visa / travel-history export is the standout:** "i-94 travel history" 4,400 (KD 34); "the us document that shows your travel history" 320; "how to check my passport travel history" 210 (KD 29); "travel history google maps" 110. *(Inference)* No app targets this, and the app's free Visa / Immigration Summary export answers it.
- **Smaller pages:** "travel expense report" 320 (KD 17) and its template 260 (KD 24); "print travel itinerary" 90; "trip budget app" 140 and "trip countdown app" 110 (both KD 37).

## Seasonality (trend data Jun 2025 – May 2026)

- Hotel cancellation searches peak in **December** (and March) → publish the hub by early November.
- Flight time calculator (Jun–Jul), itinerary templates (Jul), best travel apps (Jun–Aug) and TripIt Pro (Jun–Sep) peak in summer → publish by Feb–Mar 2027.
- Rising through 2026: travel itinerary template, travel apps, "what is itinerary travel". Falling: tripcase.

## What wins for small sites

1. First-person app listicles on small travel blogs. directiveadventure.com (AS 22) is top 10 on 6 of our results pages.
2. Competing app blogs that publish free templates and head-to-head comparisons: tripstone.app (AS 11), blueplanit.co (AS 20).
3. Explainers of competitors' pricing: tineo.ai, monkeyeatingmango.com.
4. Single-purpose calculators.
5. `/alternatives/X` pages from apps, reaching #7–9.

## Changes to wave 1 conclusions

1. **Wanderlog:** the "wanderlog vs tripit" and "wanderlog reviews/pro cost" volumes are one-month spikes. Cover pricing inside the comparison page; no standalone pricing page.
2. **Brand cancellation pages:** mostly not winnable. 6 of 8 brands are NOT_YET, and booking.com holds all 10 of its results. Build one hotel hub (plus a car-rental hub later) instead of per-brand pages.
3. **Skip** per diem and travel-expense tax queries (government results pages).
4. **Not real opportunities:** "best organization apps" is general productivity; "best road trip apps" is only partly relevant.
5. **TripCase demand is falling;** consolidate soon.
