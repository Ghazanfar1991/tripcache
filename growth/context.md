# Product context

TripCache is a cross-platform travel organizer for storing itinerary details and reminders. The public landing page and SEO blog live in this repository. The native mobile repository is deliberately out of scope.

The business goal is A$5,000 MRR. The current audited signals are early-stage and incomplete: Google Search Console shows organic impressions and clicks; GA4 currently contains app streams but no valid web stream; RevenueCat shows one active subscription while the website/store price messages disagree. Until currency-normalized RevenueCat data and a working website stream exist, conversion and MRR baselines remain provisional.

Primary markets supported by current search evidence are the United States, Australia, United Kingdom, and Canada. Claims about feature availability or pricing must match the current stores and product behavior.
