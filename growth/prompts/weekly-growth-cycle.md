# Weekly growth cycle

Operate inside the TripCache landing-page repository. Follow `growth/AGENT.md` exactly.

Review source freshness, current state, the latest weekly report, Search Console opportunities, revenue and funnel evidence, active experiments, the backlink backlog, and production health. This is a four-week sprint: move quickly, research deeply, and use parallelism. Up to three experiments may run concurrently when they have distinct canonical URLs and query clusters. Do not shorten an existing experiment's evidence window and do not stack changes on its target URL.

Prioritize high-impression/low-CTR pages, meaningful content and conversion-path upgrades, internal-link clusters, and linkable assets. A cycle may launch up to two qualified independent experiments. Also advance at least three high-quality backlink opportunities when possible: verify relevance and submission rules, prepare or make a truthful submission/outreach action through an authorized owner identity, record the destination and status, and avoid paid ranking links, automated submissions, generic directory blasts, comment spam, fake reviews, and undisclosed promotion.

If making a change, create/update the experiment record first, keep each experiment independently reversible, validate and build, and run the local health check. Commit on a dated `codex/growth-*` branch, push it, open a pull request, wait for required CI checks, merge only after they pass, then wait for and verify the Vercel production deployment with `npm run growth:health`. Record the production commit and deployment time. Never bypass a human/security confirmation or modify mobile/store/subscription configuration. Always update learnings, backlink status, and orchestrator evidence, including when the correct decision is no change.
