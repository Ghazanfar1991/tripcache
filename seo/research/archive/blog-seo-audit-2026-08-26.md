# TripCache Blog SEO Audit and Keyword Plan

Date: 2026-08-26  
Scope: 23 public blog routes imported by `lib/blog.tsx`; redirected legacy files are excluded.  
Primary evidence: Google Search Console data for 2026-07-24 through 2026-08-20, official competitor product pages, current app-store listings, and government sources where relevant.

## Executive outcome

The public portfolio now has clear query ownership, accurate comparison claims, honest editorial attribution, answer-first introductions, contextual internal links, and complete article metadata/schema. Seven high-impact articles were substantially rewritten. Narrow supporting articles received title, description, keyword, sourcing, and internal-link improvements without broadening them into competing pillar pages.

The independent blog-review gate scored the rewritten portfolio 92/100 with zero P0 or P1 findings after remediation. Organic rankings are not guaranteed; the next measurement window should focus on click-through rate, non-branded clicks, and query-to-page alignment.

## Implemented SEO and editorial changes

- Rewrote the email automation, AI planner, business travel, itinerary template, Flighty, Google replacement, and TripIt comparison articles around differentiated search intent.
- Removed fabricated statistics, obsolete prices, fake author identities, unsupported certifications, blanket security claims, and absolute parsing/accuracy promises.
- Verified current pricing and feature claims against official TripIt, Flighty, Wanderlog, Google, Tripsy, TravelPerk, Navan, Apple, and Google Play sources.
- Added concise SEO titles and descriptions where visible article headlines were too long for search snippets.
- Added contextual internal links and topic-aware related-post selection.
- Added honest organization attribution, visible editorial standards, update dates, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter tags, `BlogPosting`, `BreadcrumbList`, `CollectionPage`, and `ItemList` schema.
- Replaced misleading or technically unsuitable hero-image mappings with accurate neutral artwork and descriptive alt text.
- Preserved active experiment title/body variants for `best-travel-apps-2025`, `hotel-cancellation-reminder-app-2026`, and `tripcase-shutdown-what-now` through their recorded 2026-09-03 end date.

## Homepage and blog-index hero assessment

The homepage hero now combines category language with a benefit-led message:

- Eyebrow: “Post-booking travel organizer”
- H1: “Forward travel emails. Track cancellation deadlines.”
- Supporting copy: confirmation emails, organized itineraries, free-cancellation deadlines, travel documents, receipts, flights, stays, and expenses

This covers the researched category and workflow vocabulary without keyword stuffing. The blog index now identifies the collection as post-booking travel organization guidance rather than a generic company blog.

## Measurement plan

1. Do not judge the three active experiments before 2026-09-03.
2. Compare 28-day pre/post Search Console windows for clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, and query-page overlap.
3. Prioritize CTR work where position is already 1-5 but clicks remain near zero.
4. Watch whether “TripIt Pro pricing,” confirmation-email, document-organizer, and expense-reporting queries move toward their intended pages.
5. Add a real named expert reviewer only when a genuine accountable reviewer is available; do not fabricate one for schema.
6. Earn relevant backlinks to the itinerary template and Google replacement guides; on-page work alone will not establish authority.
