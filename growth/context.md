# Product context

TripCache is a cross-platform travel organizer for storing itinerary details and reminders. The public landing page and SEO blog live in this repository. The native mobile repository is deliberately out of scope.

The business goal is A$5,000 MRR. The current audited signals are early-stage: Search Console shows growing impressions but weak CTR; the GA4 web stream is active but has not accumulated a reliable store-intent funnel; RevenueCat reports A$11 MRR and one active subscription; and official store reporting is connected. Public pricing must remain unchanged until website, store, and RevenueCat product presentation is reconciled.

From 2026-08-17 through 2026-09-17, the owner has authorized an accelerated sprint: Monday and Thursday intelligent cycles, up to three independent page/query-cluster experiments in parallel, deeper evidence-backed page changes, and legitimate backlink outreach. The engine must still preserve attribution, reversibility, privacy, accurate claims, and Google link-spam compliance.

Primary markets supported by current search evidence are the United States, Australia, United Kingdom, and Canada. Claims about feature availability or pricing must match the current stores and product behavior.
