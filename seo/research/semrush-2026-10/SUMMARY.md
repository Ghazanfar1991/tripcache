# Semrush research summary: TripCache (trip-cache.com), October 2026

**Pulled:** 2026-10-05 · **Databases:** us, au, uk (written "gb" in the CSV), ca · **API units:** 35,470 as reported over 69 successful calls, plus 100 for the earlier list_projects call. The real spend is probably about 640 units lower (see Methodology). No limits or errors on units.

Legend: **[F]** = fact from Semrush or GSC data · **[I]** = inference or recommendation.

## Key findings
1. **[F] TripCache's footprint is tiny and almost all TripCase-related.**
   - 83 US keywords, about 12 visits/mo, Authority Score 8. 153 referring domains, mostly spam; the only real ones are the App Store, Product Hunt and dataforseo.
   - Every TripCase variant has KD 4-22. TripIt's single /web/tripcase page holds #1. Tripsy, Folio, mTrip and apurplelife also rank.
2. **[F] TripCache splits the TripCase demand across 5-6 of its own URLs.**
   - GSC shows tripcase queries on /blog/tripcase-shutdown-what-now (pos 7.5), /blog/tripcase-alternative-2025, /alternatives/tripcase, /blog, /pricing and /.
   - [I] Consolidate into /blog/tripcase-shutdown-what-now and 301 the rest. This is the quickest win available.
3. **[F] The largest relevant demand is "travel itinerary template" and its variants.**
   - "travel itinerary template" has 90.5K US / 8.1K UK / 4.4K AU / 4.4K CA searches at KD 41.
   - Google Docs and Sheets variants have 720-1.9K each at KD 10-20.
   - The SERP has Canva, Notion and Adobe, but also two small blogs and gdoc.io. [I] It is winnable with real downloadable files.
4. **[F] Comparison and competitor-pricing SERPs are soft.**
   - "wanderlog vs tripit" has 4.4K searches at KD 22. Its top 10 is forums plus three tiny app blogs.
   - "tripit pro" has 1.9K at KD 33, with third-party reviews at #3, #7 and #10.
   - "wanderlog reviews" has 12.1K at KD 31, with small blogs ranking.
5. **[F] Tools and policy pages tied to TripCache features have low KD.**
   - "flight time calculator": 3.6K, KD 31. Its top 10 is all small tool sites.
   - "hotel cancellation policy": 1K, KD 17.
   - "jet lag calculator": 1K, KD 22.
   - Brand cancellation policies: Airbnb 6.6K, Booking.com 2.9K, Vrbo 2.4K, Enterprise 2.4K, Marriott 1.6K, Hertz 1.3K; KD 33-50.
6. **[F] "best travel apps for planning" is bigger in AU (4.4K) than in the US (2.9K).**
   - GSC shows TripCache at pos ~6 with 493 impressions/28d and 0 clicks.
   - Semrush US has the same page at #42. [I] The GSC impressions are probably AU-weighted. This is a snippet/CTR fix, not a ranking problem.
7. **[F] Head terms are unwinnable for now.** Examples: "itinerary" (110K, KD 75), "trip planner" (33K, KD 75), "travel planner" (8.1K, KD 71). Wanderlog is #1-2 and TripIt is top 10.

## Methodology and limits
- **Data date:** Semrush current snapshot 2026-10-05. GSC period 2026-09-04 to 2026-10-01, read from seo/data/search-console/.
- **Reports used:**
  - domain_ranks/domain_rank, resource_organic (TripCache in 4 DBs; competitors filtered), resource_organic_unique, domain_organic_organic
  - phrase_fullsearch (volume >19), phrase_related, phrase_questions, phrase_these (US batch + AU/UK/CA for a 96-keyword shortlist), phrase_organic (13 SERPs)
  - backlinks_overview, backlinks_comparison, backlinks_refdomains, backlinks_matrix (3 gap views)
- **Unit accounting:**
  - The ledger records the returned api_units: 35,470 total.
  - Parallel calls over-reported: rows 03-05 (680 vs ~370), 07 (180 vs 70), 42-44 (600 vs 300). True spend is about 34,800.
  - Observed costs: organic positions 10/row, broad match 20/row, related and questions 40/row, phrase_these 10/row, SERP 10/row, backlink matrix and refdomains 40/row, competitors 40/row.
- **Failed calls (0 units, ledger E1-E4):**
  - domain_domains (gap) failed twice because the MCP validates display_filter against the wrong schema. The gap was rebuilt from filtered per-competitor resource_organic pulls [31-39].
  - phrase_related returned nothing for "travel document organizer app".
  - One phrase_questions call had a bad column and was retried.
- **Noisy pulls kept for completeness:** raw files 15, 21 and 36.
- **Volumes:** US volumes can differ slightly between report types; the candidates file uses the maximum. Intl volumes cover only the shortlist plus TripCache's own intl rankings. Rows with blank volume_us are non-US-only.
- **SERP features:** decoded from Semrush codes on a best-effort basis; code 52 is left as "code52".
- **Not checked:** SERPs for brand-policy pages, per diem and business-travel app; Authority Scores for SERP-derived backlink targets.
- **Read-only:** no Semrush projects were created or modified.
